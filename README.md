Quantum materials are materials that exhibit non-trivial _exotic_ properties as a direct consequence of quantum mechanics.

Supported by the NDA, my PhD focuses the behaviour of Helium in Plutonium Dioxide ([Article](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c4g49lrgje5o)). Plutonium has highly correlated electrons and exhibits rich quantum physics. I am investigating how to effectively train machine learning potentials that can accurately capture the exotic behaviour of plutonium and other strongly correlated materials.


Furthermore, I am interested in the development of the tools and algorithms to aid accurate quantum mechanical calculations, ML model accuracy and model training.
